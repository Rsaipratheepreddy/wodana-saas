Product concept · v3 · built on the firm’s process guide of 16 Oct 2026

Every job is a living map.
AI keeps it moving.

The firm’s own lead-to-won flow and its 28 design-to-warranty steps, drawn as one map per job. Staff work inside it, the client follows a simpler view of it, and AI works inside each step. Every gate in the guide blocks until its fields are there.

Material selection is not a design step

It is in no design package. It runs only when production follows (Full ID, Fit-Out & Furniture), after the renders, with the material coordinator.

Two wins, three ways to close

Proposal 1 (design fee, 100% upfront) and proposal 2 (estimate) each have their own Won date. Full ID closes at design approval; Q2 can be withdrawn; furniture closes at delivery.

After the estimate, everything is per item

Material list, technical drawing, client sign-off, margin check, release, factory and delivery are gates on each item, not on the job.

The living map

The job page is the flowchart

Lanes are seats, bands are chapters, the dark node is now. Dashed gold = automatic. Diamonds = gates. Stacked nodes = per item. Click any node, try a blocked gate, ask for a third revision, or withdraw Q2.

Before the deal

Inbox, intake and the enquiry queue

Everything before a job exists, as the guide sets it. These are steps 1 to 3 of the map, with their own rules.

WhatsApp inbox · Sales admin team + CRM owner

OpenUnreadResolvedAll
Every chat has one label: Open · Resolved · Unresolved · Spam. Only the person who resolved a chat can reopen it.
T1 · we have everything · automatic“Thank you, {{name}}! Your enquiry is now with our team…”
T2 · need more detailsForm link for that source and channel; the form sets both.
T3 · closing the chat · admin confirmsOnly after 3 working hours with no reply.
Spam: resolved silently; a spam enquiry and a flagged contact are created; later messages are marked spam.

Intake, duplicates and assignment

Required at intake: source + channel, enquiry type (TBC allowed), name, mobile with country code, emirate. Title and reference are generated and locked.
Same mobile (spaces and code ignored) or email: open lead → duplicate, linked, goes to that account manager, out of reports. Past client → returning, same account manager, never a second client record.
No match → round-robin to account managers; each sees only their own.
No activity within 1 working hour (Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00) → next account manager, both told, history kept. After a full working day → Unassigned and an email to the owner.

Why proposal 1 doesn’t go out today

Sending is blocked until every requirement is decided; the design-fee round is not exempt.
The client portal is off by default, and sending needs a decision-maker on the job.
“Sent” shows when the message is only queued; with no mail provider or worker it never leaves, and WhatsApp has no email fallback.
Five document engines; the signed PDF is not the portal design; templates keep only lines; wording is hard-coded.

In the studio

One engine for proposals, estimate, agreement, invoice, receipt, change request, handover pack and thank-you message.
Templates per round and job type; generated, locked and editable parts declared per page, as table M asks.
Each step declares what it needs before sending: the design round needs a package and a fee, not decided requirements.
Ref numbers come from sequences: estimate refs follow the quotation (rev 1, rev 2); agreement refs are automatic.

Getting there
