# Catalog foundation

Tenant flow: **Catalog → tenant-named group → records**. Groups are editable organisation, not fixed master types. A group can contain mixed record types and has an optional icon and default type for adding records. Group names and icons do not determine business behaviour.

Record IDs, record types and typed links are the integration contract. Display names, group membership and order can change without changing those IDs. A record belongs to one catalog; links stay within that catalog and workspace. Archiving retains data and is blocked when active records depend on it.

- **Room → item → variant:** rooms classify spaces; an item can reference rooms, materials, finishes, construction, execution and a measurement rule; a variant references its item. Group membership does not replace these relationships.
- **Materials:** brands, finishes, exact sizes and packs remain explicit records/specifications. A sheet or pack cannot be converted to area from its name alone.
- **Units:** dimension and a positive factor establish meaning. Base units are metre, square metre, cubic metre, kilogram, each and hour. Names/aliases are presentation. Different dimensions and product-specific packs have no universal conversion. A referenced unit's dimension/factor cannot change; create another unit. Renaming is allowed.
- **Measurement rules:** store the inputs, formula definition, output unit and limitations. Custom formula text is a specification, not executable code. The unit editor previews unit conversions only.
- **Execution:** factory/site/hybrid describes fabrication routing. Factory-made items may still need site installation. Processes have their own units, performers and applicable routes. Hybrid does not imply averaging factory and site prices or allowances.
- **Prices:** the price editor captures dated purchase, internal-cost or selling entries against one priced record and one unit. Currency, inclusions and tax treatment are explicit. Multiple supplier/date/unit entries may coexist; the catalog does not silently select a quotation price.

The shared schema is `packages/contracts/catalog-structure.json`; saves validate in `services/business-core/src/catalog/catalog.service.ts`. Browser tables and editors use the same definitions. Existing starter values and tenant edits are retained; starters are editable examples, not approved prices or commercial rules.

Quotation generation, quantity take-off, pricing calculations and downstream document/version snapshots remain separate work. Downstream modules should consume record IDs and explicit definitions, never infer behaviour from tenant group names or evaluate arbitrary stored formula text.

Imports use **Excel/CSV or paste → map columns → review → save**. Files are limited to 2 MB, and each atomic batch creates at most 500 records including generated references. The worksheet type is explicit. Unmapped columns are retained as specifications; exact matches to existing records are kept unchanged. New units require a dimension and conversion definition. Simple missing reference records are created only when selected for review. AI is not required and no workbook contents are sent to an AI service.

The import endpoint validates the full linked graph in the tenant transaction, checks versions of dependencies captured at review, and writes audit events only on commit. Review uses a savepoint and leaves no rows or audit events. Identical retries reuse record IDs; name/code conflicts, foreign-catalog links and parent cycles reject the batch. Large workbook migrations use bounded batches with stable IDs and dependency order.

The local **Room Builder Test** catalog imports all 7,392 data rows from 23 worksheets in `room_builder_catalog (4).xlsx`. The index is documentation. Original columns and source row references remain on each record. Added groups, shared references, material families and component parents produce 26 groups and 8,058 records. Source amounts appear as **Imported · needs review** and are not active price-book entries. Pack contents, missing commercial details and the two sliding-item/hinge inconsistencies remain flagged; watt/lumen/kelvin definitions are retained as custom measurement references because those conversion dimensions are unsupported.

The imported test catalog now has **27 groups and 8,151 records** after connecting its BoM. All 1,674 component rows retain their source fields and point to typed material, hardware or consumable specifications. The 243 items explicitly reference their construction templates; templates and components also reference their items. The additional Component specifications group holds 93 reference/category records derived from the workbook. These are requirements, not invented manufacturer SKUs. Exact product selection, compatible units/pack contents and commercial details still require tenant review. An audit of all active links found no missing or archived targets, foreign-catalog references, incorrect target types, duplicate single links or parent cycles.

Entry remains table-first, with paste/import and an optional detail editor. The inspector uses readable specification labels and row/section dividers, lists connected records, shows component materials, and exposes **Costing & prices** directly. **Save & set price** saves the master first and then opens its dated price entry. **Use amount** copies an explicit imported currency amount; it clears unconfirmed date, tax and inclusions. Per-pack or per-pipe prices do not inherit a material's per-litre/metre unit. Every saved price requires its own explicit unit. Import provenance and original identifiers are retained under Original import details.
